Rally Finder

This page listens to one match video and finds the rallies from the sound of the hits. It works on this phone only. Nothing is uploaded.

We want to learn three things: how fast the phone reads a long video, how well the sound shows the rallies, and whether the phone can make a 1-minute 4K cut of the best rallies.

How to record the match
  • Record in 4K, with the phone in landscape: 60 fps for slow motion (about 35 GB for 90 minutes), or 30 fps (about 15 GB). Check the setting before you start: Settings, Camera, Record Video.
  • Mount the phone on the outside of the back glass, in the centre, at shoulder height.
  • Turn on airplane mode, so that calls do not stop the recording.
  • Connect a power bank, and keep the phone in the shade.
Sample. These results come from sound that this page made. They do not come from a video.

1Choose the match video

Do these two things first. They take one minute, and save ten.

  1. Put the video in Files. In Photos, open the video, tap Share, then Save to Files. A video picked from Photos is converted first, which can take ten minutes or more. A video picked from Files is not.
  2. Keep the screen on. Settings, Display & Brightness, Auto-Lock: Never. Put the phone on the charger. If the phone locks, iOS can reload this page, and you start again.

Then tap the button, choose Browse (or "Choose File"), and pick the video in Files. Use Safari, and stay on this page until step 2 says "Done".

      4The highlights cut

      Choose what you want to watch. Each clip runs to the reaction after the point. Rallies that you marked "Not a rally" stay out.

      "Make the 4K cut" copies the original picture and sound of each rally into a new video. Nothing is re-encoded, so the cut keeps the quality of the match video. The location of the video is not copied.

        5Who hit it
